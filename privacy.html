1. Who We Are

RIK Athletic Nutrition Inc. ("RIK", "we", "us", or "our") is the data controller for personal data processed through rikathletica.com (the "Site"). Our registered address is 477 Madison Avenue, New York City, NY 10022. For any privacy question, data-subject access request (DSAR), or complaint, email bek.zhou@rikathletica.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly.

  • Contact information: name, email address, mailing address, and phone number (where requested) when you make a purchase, complete an intake or screening form, or contact support.
  • Free Race Fuel Audit form data: the inputs you supply on the audit form — including your goal race, race date, weekly training hours, GI history, sweat profile, current fueling, and any open-text notes you choose to share.
  • Sprint on-page screening data: the inputs you supply on the Sprint screening tool at rikathletica.com/sprint, including sex, age, target race name, target race date, prior race name, prior race finish time, prior race date, prior race bike split, prior race run split, and weekly training hours. We also record whether you used the autocomplete list or manually entered a race name (the target_race_manual and prior_race_manual flags), and whether your submission was flagged for manual verification because we could not match a race name against our internal list of IRONMAN-branded events (currently ~300) (the needs_verification flag).
  • Sprint full intake data: after purchase, health-adjacent information including detailed gastrointestinal history, body weight, estimated sweat rate, dietary restrictions or food allergies, current supplement use, current medications, and any disclosed medical conditions. This is collected via Typeform.
  • Sprint Check-in form data: weekly feedback you submit while running a Sprint protocol — perceived gut tolerance, energy, hydration, training response, and any open-text notes.
  • Email address: stored when you submit any form on the Site so we can deliver your audit, protocol, transactional notifications, or relevant educational content. You may unsubscribe from marketing email at any time.
  • Account-related communications: emails, support messages, and WhatsApp Business messages you send us.

Information collected automatically.

  • Usage data: pages visited, time on site, referrer URL, browser type, device type, screen size, approximate (city-level) location derived from IP, and event timestamps via Google Analytics 4 (GA4).
  • IP address: collected by our hosting provider (Vercel) for security, abuse prevention, and request routing. IP is logged transiently and is not joined with marketing data.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 12.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and deliver your Free Race Fuel Audit, Sprint screening verdict, Sprint protocol, and any other product or service you request.
  • To compute your Sprint eligibility verdict (PASS / STRETCH / UNREALISTIC) client-side from the inputs you submit; if your race name was manually entered and could not be matched against our list of recognized IRONMAN-branded events, to manually verify the race before confirming Sprint eligibility.
  • To select and customize product recommendations for your race.
  • To run weekly protocol revisions and check-ins through the Sprint program.
  • To process payments, fulfill shipments, and send order, shipping, and refund notifications.
  • To send transactional emails (audit delivery, protocol delivery, shipping notices) and, with your consent or as permitted by law, marketing emails (educational nurture sequences, race-prep content, product updates).
  • To respond to support requests via email or WhatsApp Business.
  • To improve the screening tool, the audit drafter, the protocol engine, and our product line, using aggregated and de-identified data only.
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations (tax, regulatory, court orders).

We may use anonymized and aggregated data — data that cannot reasonably be linked to any individual — for internal research, methodology refinement, and product development.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): to deliver the Free Race Fuel Audit, the Sprint screening verdict, the Sprint protocol service, the Bundle, and related transactional communications you have requested or purchased.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): for marketing emails, non-essential cookies, and any processing of special-category health-adjacent data (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): for analytics, security and fraud prevention, methodology improvement using aggregated data, and direct communication with existing customers about closely related products. We balance these interests against your rights and freedoms and do not rely on legitimate interests where your rights override.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): for tax, accounting, and regulatory record-keeping.

5. Payment Processing (Stripe)

All payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not store your full payment-card number, CVV, or expiry on our servers. When you check out, you are routed to a Stripe-hosted page or Stripe Elements iframe; payment-card data is transmitted directly to Stripe. We receive a transaction ID, the last 4 digits of your card, your billing name, and a tokenized customer reference. Stripe's privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data with the following service providers — and only the data needed to deliver the named function. Each provider is contractually bound to use the data only for the purpose described.

  • Stripe (payment processing). Receives card and billing data directly from your browser to charge your payment method and issue refunds.
  • ShipWizard (3PL fulfillment). Receives your name, shipping address, phone number (for carrier handoff), and order line items to pick, pack, and ship physical orders.
  • Dropbox Sign (HelloSign — e-signature). Receives your name, email, and the service-agreement document to collect a signed Sprint service agreement.
  • Typeform (forms). Hosts our intake, screening, and check-in forms. Your form responses are routed to RIK and stored in our backend; Typeform retains a copy for the duration of its retention policy.
  • Kit (formerly ConvertKit — email marketing). Receives your email address, first name, opt-in source, and a small set of profile fields (e.g., race date, distance, tier) so we can send transactional and educational email and apply nurture-sequence logic. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Gmail SMTP (Google). Used as our transactional email transport for audit delivery and admin alerts. Receives recipient address and message contents only for delivery.
  • Anthropic (Claude API — AI-assisted audit drafting). See Section 8. Used internally by RIK to generate a draft analysis from your audit form inputs. Your draft is reviewed and edited by the founder before any audit is delivered. Anthropic does not train its models on inputs or outputs submitted via its API (as of the "Last updated" date of this Policy).
  • Vercel (hosting). Hosts the Site and serverless functions on US infrastructure. Processes request logs and IP addresses for routing, security, and abuse prevention.
  • Vercel Postgres (database). Stores audit form submissions, screening submissions, and other backend records. Encrypted at rest.
  • Vercel Blob (object storage). Stores the generated audit PDF deliverables. Access is restricted to authenticated and time-limited URLs.
  • Google Analytics 4 (analytics). Receives anonymized usage data: pages, sessions, referrers, device, and event metrics. We use IP-anonymization where supported and have not enabled Google Signals or ads-personalization.
  • Secure Privacy CMP (cookie consent). Loads our cookie banner and stores your consent choices in your browser. The CMP receives your consent state and a pseudonymous identifier.
  • Rewardful (affiliate tracking). Sets a first-party cookie when you arrive via an affiliate link, identifying the referring affiliate so commissions are calculated correctly. See Section 9.
  • WhatsApp Business (Meta — async support). If you message us via WhatsApp (Sprint customers during the 4-week protocol window), Meta processes the message under its own privacy policy and infrastructure. We retain only what we need to support you. Do not share clinical or insurance information via WhatsApp.
  • jsDelivr CDN (static assets). Delivers static images and assets from a public CDN. jsDelivr may log IP and user-agent transiently for delivery and abuse prevention; no personal account data is shared with jsDelivr by us.

We do not share audit form data, screening submissions, intake data, protocol contents, or any health-adjacent data with any party other than the providers above, and only for the purpose described.

8. AI-Assisted Audit Analysis (Anthropic Claude)

We use the Anthropic Claude API internally to generate a draft analysis of your Free Race Fuel Audit form inputs. Specifically:

  • Your audit form inputs are sent to Anthropic's API to produce a written draft.
  • The founder (Bek Zhou) reviews and edits every draft before any audit is delivered to you.
  • The audit you receive is a human-reviewed deliverable. We do not run an autonomous AI customer agent.

Anthropic's commercial API does not use customer inputs or outputs to train its models by default (as of the "Last updated" date of this Policy); we have not opted into any training program. Anthropic processes API data as a service provider and retains it only as long as needed to operate and secure the API. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy and Commercial Terms for details.

If you would prefer that we not pass your audit form inputs through Anthropic's API, email bek.zhou@rikathletica.com before submitting; we will run your audit manually instead.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required by law.

  • Free Race Fuel Audit data: 24 months from submission, then deleted from active systems.
  • Sprint screening submissions: 24 months from submission, then deleted from active systems. Submissions flagged for manual race verification (needs_verification: true) are retained for the same period.
  • Sprint intake, protocol, and check-in data: for the duration of the program plus 12 months, then deleted from active systems.
  • Order and transaction records: retained as long as required for tax, accounting, and warranty purposes (typically 7 years), then deleted or anonymized.
  • Aggregated and de-identified analytics: retained indefinitely for product research and methodology improvement, since this data is no longer linked to any individual.
  • Backups: may persist for up to 90 days after deletion from active systems, after which they are overwritten in normal rotation.

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing bek.zhou@rikathletica.com. We will process verified deletion requests within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
